After the application is approved and the documents are signed, the system allocates an IBAN and creates the customer profile. The applicant receives an in-app notification and a message at the validated email address. A message confirming receipt of the application must not be confused with approval; the account can be used only after an explicit activation notice.

At the first sign-in, the customer confirms the phone number, creates an application PIN, and may enable the device's biometric authentication. The application PIN is different from the card PIN. The bank does not ask the customer to disclose either code by phone, email, or chat.

An active account can receive incoming payments once the IBAN is displayed. Some functions may remain restricted until the first sign-in is completed and the device is confirmed. Transfers to new beneficiaries, changes to contact details, and activation of an additional device may require strong authentication.

If a card was ordered, a virtual card becomes available only for products that include this option. The physical card is produced after account approval and delivered to the confirmed address or selected collection point. The customer should verify their name and address before signing; a later change may delay delivery.

An initial deposit is not required to activate the standard personal account. However, functions involving a payment or fee cannot be completed without sufficient funds. If activation does not appear within 30 minutes after the approval message, the customer should close and reopen the application, then check the connection and installed version before contacting support.
