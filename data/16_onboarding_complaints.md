This synthetic document describes an invented complaint process for the training corpus.

An applicant may complain about delays, unclear document requests, accessibility problems, an incorrect fee, staff conduct, or technical failures in the onboarding flow.

The complaint should include the application reference, a description of the problem, the date it occurred, the requested outcome, and any relevant screenshots that do not expose full identity-document data.

A complaint is acknowledged within two business days. A final response is normally issued within fifteen business days. When additional investigation is required, the applicant receives a progress update and a revised target date.

The reviewer may inspect application events, communications, submitted evidence, versioned eligibility rules, and the fee schedule effective on the submission date. An incorrectly charged synthetic fee may be refunded.

The complaint process cannot replace identity verification, bypass sanctions controls, or make an unsupported applicant eligible. Filing a complaint does not guarantee account approval.

A complaint concerning a 2025 application must use the 2025 eligibility and fee documents. A complaint concerning an application submitted from 1 January 2026 must use the 2026 versions.
