An application may be rejected when identity cannot be verified, information is incomplete or contradictory, documents cannot be validated, the beneficial owner is not identified, the source of funds is not adequately explained, or the relationship risk cannot be accepted. The bank may refuse to start the relationship without disclosing details that would undermine security controls, fraud prevention, or legal obligations.

The decision notice states whether the applicant may correct the application, submit a new application, or request reconsideration. Reconsideration is available only when there is new information or a possible factual error. General disagreement with the decision, without additional evidence, does not cause the review to be reopened.

A reconsideration request must be sent through the secure channel indicated in the application within 10 calendar days of the decision. The applicant identifies the application and attaches the relevant evidence. Only one reconsideration is permitted for the same application. A response is normally communicated within 5 working days, although external checks may extend the period.

A complaint concerning application operation, staff conduct, or clarity of information is different from reconsideration of the decision. It may be submitted through the bank's official channels and should describe the date, affected step, and observed result. Passwords, PINs, and authorisation codes must not be included.

Submitting a complaint does not activate the account and does not automatically pause application expiry. If a confirmed technical error caused the issue, the bank may allow the affected step to be repeated without an additional fee. A new application is assessed under the rules and fees effective on its new start date.
