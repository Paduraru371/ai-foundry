This schedule applies to personal applications started between 1 January 2025 and 14 January 2026 inclusive. The date on which the application was started determines the applicable fee version, even if the review is completed later.

| Onboarding service | 2025 fee |
|---|---:|
| Opening a standard personal account | 0 lei |
| Standard video identification | 0 lei |
| Ordinary manual review | 0 lei |
| Priority review requested by the customer | 35 lei |
| Review of a document written in a foreign language | 60 lei/document |
| Standard card delivery in Romania | 0 lei |
| Repeat delivery after an incorrect address was declared | 20 lei |
| Paper account confirmation issued at opening | 15 lei |

Priority review reduces the applicant's place in the queue but does not remove compliance checks and does not guarantee approval. The fee is due only when the option is available and the customer confirms it before the review starts.

The fee for reviewing a foreign-language document applies only to documents that the bank agrees to review without an authorised translation. When an authorised translation is required, its cost is borne separately by the applicant and is not included in this fee.

No fee is charged for correcting a technical error caused by the system or for uploading a document again because an image was unclear. Card fees and transaction fees after activation are set out in the documents for the selected product, not in this schedule.

This version must not be used for applications started on or after 15 January 2026.
