This synthetic fee schedule applies to personal applications submitted from 1 January 2026.

| Onboarding service | Standard online application | Branch-assisted application |
|---|---:|---:|
| Account-opening fee | 0 RON | 0 RON |
| Optional staff assistance | Not applicable | 25 RON |
| Foreign identity-document review | 1% of initial funding, capped at 100 RON | 1% of initial funding, capped at 100 RON |
| Additional same-day liveness review | 15 RON | Not applicable |
| Replacement identification link | 0 RON | 0 RON |

The foreign identity-document review fee is exactly 1% of the initial funding amount, with a maximum charge of 100 RON. For an initial funding amount of 2,000 RON, the fee is 20 RON. For 15,000 RON, the fee is capped at 100 RON.

Optional staff assistance is charged only when the applicant is eligible for standard online onboarding but asks to complete the process with staff support. It is not charged when branch attendance is required by the eligibility or identity policy.

The additional same-day liveness review is optional. An applicant may instead wait for the free standard retry window.

Initial funding is not an onboarding fee. The required minimum is contained in the effective eligibility document.

The application submission date selects the fee version. A draft created in 2025 but submitted in 2026 uses this schedule.

A correct answer about total onboarding requirements must combine this fee schedule with the 2026 eligibility document. This file alone does not say whether an applicant qualifies.
