This synthetic policy is used when the automated address check does not pass or the identity document does not contain a current residential address.

Accepted address evidence includes a utility invoice issued within the last three months, a bank-account statement issued within the last three months, a tax-authority letter issued within the last six months, a signed residential lease that is currently valid, or a local-government certificate issued within the last three months.

A mobile-telephone invoice is not accepted. A document downloaded as a PDF is accepted when it clearly shows the issuer, applicant name, full address, and issue date. A screenshot of an account page is not accepted.

The applicant name must match the onboarding application. Initials may be accepted where the family name matches and no conflicting identity information exists. A document addressed only to another household member does not prove the applicant's residence.

An applicant living with a parent or partner may use a host declaration. The host supplies one accepted proof-of-address document, and the applicant supplies a government, education, or employer letter issued within the last six months. The case is sent to manual review.

Temporary accommodation requires manual review. Hotel confirmations, short-term holiday bookings, postal boxes, and mail-forwarding services are insufficient.

The address document verifies residence only. It does not replace the primary identity document. An assistant must not claim that a utility invoice proves identity.

When two address documents contain different addresses, the application remains pending until the applicant explains the difference and confirms the current residence.
