The standard online flow requires the original identity document. A photograph of a copy, a document displayed on another screen, or a printed image is not accepted. The document must be valid on the application date, legible, undamaged, and suitable for checking its security features and the holder's photograph.

For a Romanian identity card, the applicant photographs the front and, when requested by the application, the back. For an accepted national identity document issued by another country, every side containing information must be uploaded. If the document does not show the home address, the applicant must also provide proof of address issued within the previous 90 days. Accepted evidence includes bank statements, utility bills, residence certificates, or documents issued by a public authority, provided that the full name and address are visible.

A passport may be used only in flows where the application explicitly lists it as an accepted document. A driving licence, employee badge, birth certificate, or health card does not replace an identity document.

Images must be in colour, without glare, strong shadows, fingers covering information, or cropped edges. Manually uploaded files must be PDF, JPG, or PNG and no larger than 10 MB each. Documents written in a language other than Romanian or English may require an authorised translation.

If the applicant's current name differs from the name shown in other supplied documents, the applicant must upload evidence of the change, such as a marriage certificate or administrative decision. The bank may request additional documents when information cannot be confirmed from independent sources.
