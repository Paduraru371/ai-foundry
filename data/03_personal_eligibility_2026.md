This synthetic document applies to applications submitted from 1 January 2026 and supersedes the 2025 eligibility version.

An applicant must be at least 18 years old and must provide an accepted, unexpired identity document. The applicant must have a verified mobile number and email address and must provide a residential address in Romania or another European Union member state.

Digital onboarding is available to applicants with up to two declared tax residences. Applicants with three or more tax residences require branch-assisted onboarding and enhanced tax review.

The minimum initial funding amount is 100 RON. It must be transferred from an account in the applicant's own name. For a joint application, the initial transfer may come from an account held by either applicant.

A customer may hold up to three active personal current accounts in this synthetic product family. This limit changed from two to three on 1 January 2026. Each joint account counts toward the limit of both holders.

Applications remain valid for 45 calendar days. If the applicant does not provide requested evidence by the expiry date, a new application must be created.

Eligibility does not determine the total cost of onboarding. Identity-review and optional assisted-service fees are defined in the current fee schedule. A complete response must combine this document with the fee schedule effective on the application submission date.

Applicants are not automatically rejected because a case enters manual review. A case may be approved, declined, or returned for additional evidence after review.
