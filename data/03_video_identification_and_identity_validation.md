Remote identification confirms that the person completing the application is the holder of the presented document. The process combines document data extraction, authenticity checks, facial comparison, and a live-presence video sequence. For eligible Romanian documents, the information may be compared in real time with official data available to the bank.

Before recording, the applicant must allow the application to access the camera and microphone. The face must be fully visible, without sunglasses, a mask, a helmet, or a video filter. Light should come from the front and no other person should appear in the background. The application may ask the applicant to turn their head, read a short sequence of digits, or move the document closer to the camera. The order of actions may vary to prevent the use of a previously recorded video.

Verification fails when the image is blurred, the face is partly covered, the document appears altered, the connection is interrupted, or there is a significant difference between the applicant and the document photograph. An unsuccessful result does not automatically mean that the application is rejected; another attempt may be allowed under the retry policy effective on the application date.

In some cases, the automated result is sent to an operator for manual review. The operator checks only the elements required for identification and may request that an action be repeated or that a new image be uploaded. The applicant must never send passwords, authorisation codes, PINs, or card images.

Recordings and verification evidence are linked to the application and retained under customer due diligence and data-protection requirements. The account is not activated until identity verification has an accepted result.
