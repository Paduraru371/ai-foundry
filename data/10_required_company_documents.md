For a Romanian company, the standard flow requires the unique identification code, the identity document of the administrator or legal representative, and the identity documents of individuals who directly or indirectly hold more than 20% of the ownership interests. Every uploaded image must be in colour, legible, and show the complete document.

When public data is complete and current, the registration certificate and basic information may be retrieved automatically. The bank may request an updated constitutional document, company-status certificate, decision appointing the administrator, beneficial-owner declaration, or documents explaining the ownership chain. The documents must be consistent with one another and with the consulted registers.

For liberal professions, the identity document, tax identification code, and evidence of the right to practise are required. Depending on the profession, this may be an authorisation, membership certificate, registration decision, or document issued by the professional body.

If the representative does not appear in the registers with sufficient authority, a mandate or power of attorney must be supplied in the required form. It must identify the company, the authorised person, and the account-opening operation. The bank may require inspection of the original or a qualified electronic signature.

Documents issued abroad may require an apostille, legalisation, and authorised translation. An expired document, a screenshot from a register, or a black-and-white file is not sufficient. For a structure with several levels, documents must be supplied for each level until the individuals who ultimately control the company are identified.
