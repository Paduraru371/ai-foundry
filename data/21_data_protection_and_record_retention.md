The onboarding process handles identity data, contact details, document images, video recordings, tax, professional, and financial information, company information, beneficial-owner data, and compliance-check results. The information is used for identity verification, application assessment, fraud prevention, legal compliance, and entering into the contract.

Access is provided only to people and service providers with a role in the process and is limited to the information they require. Technical providers may process images, device signals, and biometric results under the bank's instructions and contractual security safeguards. The final decision concerning the relationship remains with the bank.

An unsigned application may be retained for the period required to resume the flow and manage incidents. Identification evidence and documents from an approved relationship are retained under the legal periods applicable to customer due diligence and contractual records. A deletion request cannot remove information that the bank is legally required to retain.

The applicant should use their own device or another trusted device, avoid sending documents through unauthorised channels, and check application permissions. Video identification should not be completed in the presence of other people or in a place where confidential information appears in the background.

Data errors should be reported before signing. After activation, information is updated through the dedicated function or the channel indicated by the bank. Technical logs may retain the date, IP address, device type, and result of the steps for security and audit purposes, but they do not replace document verification.
