A person living outside Romania or holding tax residence in several countries may start an online application only when the document type and country are available in the application list. Availability of a country does not mean automatic approval; every tax residence and document must be verified.

The applicant declares the home address, correspondence address, every country of tax residence, and the relevant tax identification numbers. If a number has not been issued, the applicable reason is selected and evidence may be requested. When an address is not shown on the identity document, proof of address issued within the previous 90 days is required.

Foreign documents must be valid, complete, and photographed in their original form. The bank may request a passport, national identity document, residence permit, tax residence certificate, or evidence of income source. Documents not written in Romanian or English may require an authorised translation or a separately charged language review.

US tax status, a US address or telephone number, and other US tax indicators must be declared. The application may request additional tax forms before signing.

A non-resident application often enters manual review and may take longer than the standard automated process. To determine the total cost, this document must be read together with the applicable fee schedule. From 15 January 2026, accepted review of a foreign-language document costs 75 lei for each document in the personal flow, provided that an authorised translation is not required.
