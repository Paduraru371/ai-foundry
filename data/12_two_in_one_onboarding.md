This synthetic document models a combined flow inspired only by the public name of a two-in-one onboarding journey. The operational rules are invented.

An authorised representative may apply for one personal account and one business account in a linked application. The linked flow creates two separate application records under one journey reference.

The applicant completes contact verification and video identification once. The identity result may be reused by both applications for 30 calendar days, provided that the identity document remains valid and no personal data changes.

Reuse of identity verification does not mean that eligibility is shared. The personal application must satisfy the personal eligibility policy. The company application must independently satisfy the business eligibility policy.

The personal account may be approved while the business account remains in manual review. The applicant may choose to activate the approved personal account without waiting for the business result.

Fees are calculated separately. Personal onboarding charges come from the personal fee schedule. Business charges come from the business fee schedule. A fee waiver in one application does not automatically apply to the other.

The initial funding transfers must be separate and must use the correct payment reference. The personal transfer must come from an account in the individual's name. The business transfer must come from the company, an authorised founder account accepted by the business policy, or another approved source.

If the shared video-identification result expires before one application is submitted, only that application requires a new identification session.

A complete answer about the linked journey may require four documents: personal eligibility, personal fees, business eligibility, and business fees.
