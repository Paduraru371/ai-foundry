This long synthetic procedure is designed to test chunking and step preservation.

1. The applicant opens the secure identification link and enters the application reference. The link is valid for 24 hours.

2. The applicant verifies the email address using a six-character code. The code expires after ten minutes.

3. Camera and microphone access are enabled. Existing gallery files cannot be uploaded.

4. The applicant selects the identity-document type and reads the capture instructions.

5. The front and back of the document are photographed where applicable. All corners must remain visible.

6. The system extracts the legal name, date of birth, document number, issuing country, and expiry date. The applicant confirms or corrects the extracted data.

7. The applicant records a liveness video. The screen displays three random digits that must be read aloud while the applicant slowly turns their head.

8. The facial image is compared with the document photograph. A low-confidence result creates a manual-review task and is not an automatic rejection.

9. The applicant confirms citizenship, tax residence, occupation, source of funds, and expected monthly transaction range.

10. The applicant reviews the application summary and corrects errors before submission.

11. The system returns one of three statuses: verified, manual review, or action required.

12. When action is required, the applicant receives a message naming the missing item without disclosing internal risk scoring.

Two unsuccessful attempts are allowed. After the third unsuccessful attempt, video identification is locked for exactly 24 hours. The applicant may wait and retry or select branch-assisted onboarding.

The order of these steps matters. The application cannot proceed to contract acceptance before identity verification and compliance screening are complete.
