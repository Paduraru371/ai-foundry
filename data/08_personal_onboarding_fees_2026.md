This schedule applies to personal applications started on or after 15 January 2026. When an earlier saved application has expired, a new application uses the fees effective on the date it is restarted.

| Onboarding service | 2026 fee |
|---|---:|
| Opening a standard personal account | 0 lei |
| Standard video identification | 0 lei |
| Ordinary manual review | 0 lei |
| Priority review requested by the customer | 45 lei |
| Review of a document written in a foreign language | 75 lei/document |
| Standard card delivery in Romania | 0 lei |
| Repeat delivery after an incorrect address was declared | 25 lei |
| Digital account confirmation issued at opening | 0 lei |
| Paper account confirmation issued at opening | 20 lei |

Priority review is available only for complete applications that do not require beneficial-owner verification, high-risk approval, or sanctions clarification. Paying for the option does not change the acceptance standards and is not refunded when an application is rejected because of information supplied by the applicant.

The exact fee for reviewing a foreign-language document is **75 lei for each document**. A PDF containing several distinct documents is charged separately for each identified document. Authorised translations, legalisation, and apostilles are obtained by the applicant at their own expense.

No fee is charged for standard opening, video verification, correcting data before signing, or uploading a document requested by the bank. Costs incurred after activation are governed by the fee schedule for the selected account and card.

The 2025 version is historical and must not be used for new applications.
