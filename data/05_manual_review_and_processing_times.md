An application enters manual review when automated systems cannot fully confirm the identity, documents, or declared profile. Common situations include photographs with glare, differences between the declared address and uploaded evidence, multiple tax residencies, complex ownership structures, name matches on sanctions lists, or expected transaction values that appear unusual for the declared occupation.

The standard time for the first manual review is no more than **2 working days** from the moment the application contains all requested documents. The period is paused while the bank is waiting for a document or answer from the applicant. Applications received on non-working days are treated as entering review on the next working day.

Additional requests appear in the application and identify the exact document or information required. Unless the message specifies another deadline, the applicant has 5 calendar days to respond. Short explanations and relevant supporting documents are accepted; there is no need to upload large amounts of unrelated information.

An individual may be asked for proof of address, evidence of income source, or clarification of tax residence. A company may be asked for an updated constitutional document, beneficial-owner evidence, the representative's mandate, or an explanation of its economic activity.

Manual review is not provisional approval. The IBAN and payment services are activated only after the final decision. Support may communicate the general status of an application, but it cannot remove checks, change verification results, or promise an exact approval time.
