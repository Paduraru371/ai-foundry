Personal online account opening is completed in the following order. The steps must be completed within the same application; returning to the application is allowed only until it expires.

1. **Start the application.** The applicant selects the personal product and confirms that a new banking relationship is requested.
2. **Validate the phone number.** The number is confirmed with a one-time code. It cannot be linked to another active application containing different identity data.
3. **Validate the email address.** A second code confirms the address used for notifications and documents.
4. **Initial consents.** The applicant reviews the digital-channel terms and the data-processing notice.
5. **Personal information.** The applicant enters their name, home address, citizenship, marital status, and identity-document data. Automatically extracted fields must be checked.
6. **Tax residence.** Every country of tax residence and the corresponding tax identification number are declared when available.
7. **Professional profile.** The applicant declares occupation, employer, business sector, and main source of income.
8. **Purpose of the account.** The applicant selects the primary use: receiving income, current payments, saving, or permitted self-employed activity.
9. **Transaction profile.** The applicant estimates the number and value of monthly incoming payments, outgoing payments, international transfers, and cash transactions.
10. **Upload the document.** The original identity document is photographed according to the instructions.
11. **Video identification.** The live-presence sequence and facial comparison are completed.
12. **Compliance checks.** The application is screened for sanctions, public exposure, data consistency, and fraud risks.
13. **Choose the configuration.** The applicant selects the account currency, card type, and available delivery option.
14. **Review contractual documents.** The fee summary, framework agreement, and declarations are displayed before signing.
15. **Electronic signature.** The documents are confirmed using a one-time code and timestamp.
16. **Decision.** The application is automatically approved, referred for manual review, or rejected.
17. **Activation.** After approval, an IBAN is allocated, application access is activated, and the card steps are communicated separately.

When a discrepancy is identified, the applicant should correct only the indicated field. Repeatedly creating new applications may trigger a temporary block of the onboarding flow.
