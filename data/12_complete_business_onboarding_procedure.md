Business onboarding follows a controlled sequence because approval depends on both the entity information and verification of the associated individuals.

1. The representative enters a phone number and email address and validates both channels.
2. The representative selects the legal form and enters the unique identification code.
3. The system retrieves the company name, registered office, status, administrators, and available business activities.
4. The representative confirms the information or flags data that is no longer current.
5. The actual activity, purpose of the account, start date of activity, and main products or services are declared.
6. The applicant estimates turnover, monthly incoming and outgoing payments, cash use, and the countries of counterparties.
7. The source of the initial funds and the origin of the company's revenue are described.
8. Direct shareholders and every intermediate level of ownership are declared.
9. Beneficial owners and the nature of their control are identified.
10. Representatives, authorised persons, and people who will receive application permissions are added.
11. The company documents and required identity documents are uploaded.
12. The legal representative completes video identification. Other people may receive separate invitations.
13. The system checks sanctions, public exposure, jurisdictions, activity, and information consistency.
14. The applicant selects the package, account currencies, cards, and payment-authorisation rules.
15. The applicable fees and contractual documents are displayed.
16. The representative electronically signs the declarations and contracts. Under joint representation, each signatory completes their own step.
17. The application is approved, referred for manual review, or returned for completion.
18. After approval, the IBANs are allocated, the business profile is created, and authorised persons are activated.
19. Cards and additional devices are issued according to the approved configuration.
20. The first sign-in confirms the device and the payment-signing rules.

An application is not complete while a response from an invited person is outstanding. A change in the ownership structure during review requires every affected declaration to be updated.
