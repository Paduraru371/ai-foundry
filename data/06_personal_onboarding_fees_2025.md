This synthetic fee schedule applies only to applications submitted during 2025. It is intentionally similar to the 2026 schedule but contains different values.

| Onboarding service | Standard online application | Branch-assisted application |
|---|---:|---:|
| Account-opening fee | 0 RON | 0 RON |
| Optional staff assistance | Not applicable | 15 RON |
| Foreign identity-document review | 20 RON | 20 RON |
| Additional same-day liveness review | 10 RON | Not applicable |
| Replacement identification link | 0 RON | 0 RON |

The optional staff-assistance fee applied only when an applicant who was eligible for the online flow chose branch assistance for convenience. It did not apply when the bank required a branch visit.

The foreign identity-document review fee was a fixed 20 RON per application, regardless of the initial funding amount. It was charged once even when the document had to be captured again because of image quality.

A same-day liveness review could be requested after two failed automated attempts. Waiting for the standard retry window was free.

The minimum initial funding amount was not a fee. It remained the customer's money and was defined in the 2025 eligibility document.

Fee selection used the submission date, not the date on which the draft was created. A draft started in December 2025 but submitted in January 2026 had to use the 2026 schedule.

This schedule must not be combined with 2026 eligibility rules when answering a historical 2025 question.
