This synthetic procedure is intentionally long so that a naive chunker may separate dependent steps.

1. An authorised representative enters a mobile number and email address and verifies both channels.

2. The representative enters the legal name, registration number, legal form, registered office, correspondence address, and main activity of the entity.

3. The system requests expected annual turnover, expected monthly payments, main trading countries, source of initial funds, and purpose of the account.

4. Directors, representatives, and beneficial owners are declared. Ownership percentages and control rights must total consistently.

5. The applicant uploads registration and authority documents. Expired certificates or incomplete pages create an action-required status.

6. Each required natural person receives a separate identification link. One person cannot complete video identification for another.

7. Screening is performed for the entity and all relevant natural persons. Potential matches are sent to manual review.

8. The ownership structure is compared with submitted documents and available registers. Differences must be explained.

9. The package and optional services are selected. Fees must be taken from the schedule effective on the submission date.

10. Draft contractual documents are generated. The authorised signers review and accept them.

11. The initial funding transfer is requested only after preliminary approval.

12. The application receives an approved, declined, action-required, or manual-review status.

The steps must be completed in order where a dependency exists. Contract acceptance cannot replace beneficial-owner verification, and initial funding cannot replace an unresolved compliance check.

An application is not active merely because one representative has completed video identification.
