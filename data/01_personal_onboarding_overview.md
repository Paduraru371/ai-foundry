This is a synthetic training document created for a fictional knowledge-base exercise using the public name Libra Internet Bank. The operational rules below are invented and must not be treated as real bank policy.

The Libra Online Personal Account onboarding journey has six stages: application creation, contact verification, identity verification, eligibility assessment, compliance review, and contract acceptance. A submitted form is not the same as an approved account.

The applicant starts by entering a mobile number and email address. Both channels must be verified before personal information is collected. The applicant then provides their legal name, date of birth, citizenship, tax residence, home address, occupation, expected account use, and expected source of incoming funds.

Identity is normally checked through a video-identification flow. The applicant captures an accepted identity document, records a short liveness video, and confirms that the application data is correct. Cases with unclear images, inconsistent data, or unsuccessful automated checks are sent to manual review.

A complete personal application usually receives a decision within one business day. A manual review may take up to five business days after all requested evidence is received. The time target is not a guarantee.

The account becomes active only after the applicant accepts the contractual documents and the final compliance checks are completed. An application reference proves only that a file exists.

Eligibility conditions are maintained in a separate versioned document. Charges are maintained in a separate fee schedule. Any answer about whether an applicant can open the account and what the process costs must combine the effective eligibility document with the effective fee document.
