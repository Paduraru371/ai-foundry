This synthetic document defines the statuses used across the corpus.

Draft means the applicant has started but has not submitted the application. No eligibility decision has been made.

Submitted means the required form has been sent, but identity, compliance, or document checks may still be incomplete.

Action required means the applicant must provide a named document, repeat a capture, correct information, or answer an additional question.

Manual review means an employee must assess the case. It is not equivalent to rejection.

Preliminarily approved means the eligibility and identity checks have passed, but contract acceptance or initial funding is still outstanding.

Active means all required checks, contracts, and activation conditions are complete.

Declined means the application cannot proceed. Closed means the application was withdrawn, duplicated, or expired without a decision on eligibility.

A personal application submitted from 1 January 2026 expires after 45 calendar days. A 2025 personal application expired after 30 days. A business application expires after 60 days.

Supplying one requested document does not reset the expiry period. A reviewer may grant one extension of exactly 15 calendar days when the delay results from a document issued by a public authority.

An expired application cannot be reactivated. A new application must be created, and the eligibility and fee versions effective on the new submission date apply.

An application reference does not prove approval or account activation.
