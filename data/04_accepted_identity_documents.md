This is a synthetic list created for retrieval testing. It does not describe the real acceptance policy of Libra Internet Bank.

The standard online flow accepts one primary identity document. The document must be captured live through the onboarding interface. Screenshots, scanned printouts, photocopies, and photographs opened from another screen are not accepted.

Accepted documents in this training corpus are a Romanian identity card, a Romanian electronic identity card, a Romanian passport, an EU national identity card with a machine-readable zone, or an EU passport. A temporary paper identity document requires branch-assisted onboarding.

The document must be unexpired on the date of submission. All edges must be visible, text must be readable, and reflections must not cover the photograph, document number, or expiry date.

The legal name entered in the application must match the document. The omission of a second given name may be sent to manual review. A different surname requires supporting evidence such as a marriage certificate, final divorce document, or legal name-change document.

The identity photograph must be suitable for facial comparison. A damaged document may be rejected even when the printed expiry date is still valid.

An identity document and proof of residential address are separate requirements. A Romanian identity card may satisfy both only when its printed address is current and matches the application. A passport confirms identity but does not independently confirm the residential address.

Staff and assistants must not invent alternative documents. Employee cards, student cards, social-media profiles, library cards, and photographs of bank cards are not accepted identity evidence in this synthetic policy.
