This policy applies to applications started on or after 15 January 2026 and replaces the 2025 version.

An applicant may complete no more than **3 video-identification attempts within a 24-hour period**. An attempt is recorded after the live-presence sequence starts. Closing the application, refusing camera permission after starting, or deliberately interrupting the process does not cancel the attempt.

After three unsuccessful results, identification is blocked for 24 hours from the last attempt. Support cannot manually reset the counter. If the platform records an internal technical error before capturing data, the attempt is removed automatically and the user receives a retry message.

The application remains active for **15 calendar days** from the start date. Additional requests sent by the bank do not automatically extend the period; the message may provide a separate deadline for the requested document. If the period expires before signing, the applicant starts a new application and accepts the current documents and fees.

The reduction from five to three attempts and from 30 to 15 days took effect on 15 January 2026. Search systems should use the document date and status to avoid answers based on the old policy.

An identity document that expires before the final decision must be replaced. Uploading a replacement document does not reset the number of video attempts or extend the 15-day period.
