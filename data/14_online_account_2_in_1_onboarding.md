Online Account 2 in 1 allows an eligible representative to request a company account and a personal account within the same flow. The two components are separate products with different IBANs, contractual documents, fees, and purposes. Company funds must not be used as personal funds, and the personal account does not replace the company's financial records.

The applicant first completes the contact information and performs one video identification. Confirmed identity data is reused for the applicant's role as a personal customer and as the company representative. The KYC information is not identical, however: the personal component requires occupation, income, and personal purpose, while the business component requires company activity, ownership structure, beneficial owners, and transaction profile.

The two components may have different outcomes. The personal account may be approved while the business component remains under review for additional documents. Rejection of one component does not automatically cancel the other, except where the reason affects identity, sanctions, or the integrity of the supplied information.

Contractual documents are signed separately, even when they appear in the same session. The applicant must check the product named at the top of each document before signing.

The first video identification is included in the flow. If another person must be authorised for the company, that person receives an invitation and completes a separate verification. Fees for additional representatives and foreign documents are determined under the business fee schedule, not the personal-account fees.
