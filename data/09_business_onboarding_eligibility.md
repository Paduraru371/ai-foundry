This synthetic document describes training-only eligibility rules for an online business-account flow associated with the public Libra Internet Bank name.

Eligible applicants are Romanian limited-liability companies, authorised natural persons, individual enterprises, family enterprises, and Romanian professional practices. The entity must be active, have a Romanian registration or professional authorisation, and provide a Romanian correspondence address.

All directors, authorised representatives, and beneficial owners holding or controlling at least 25% must be declared. At least one authorised representative must complete video identification.

Standard online onboarding is available only where the ownership chain has no more than two corporate layers and all direct or indirect owners can be documented. Foundations, associations, trusts, public companies, virtual-asset exchanges, gambling operators, and entities with bearer ownership are outside the standard synthetic flow.

The company must provide its activity description, expected annual turnover, expected monthly transaction volume, main payment countries, source of initial funds, and tax-residence information.

The minimum initial funding amount is 500 RON for the Start package and 2,500 RON for the Growth package. These amounts are eligibility conditions, not charges.

An application remains valid for 60 calendar days. Missing beneficial-owner evidence, unresolved screening matches, or unexplained ownership discrepancies may result in closure of the application.

Eligibility does not determine fees. The current business onboarding fee schedule must be consulted. A complete answer about a company application requires both documents.
