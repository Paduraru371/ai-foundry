The online business flow is intended for registered and active companies and professional forms for which the legal representative can act independently and complete video identification. The business must have up-to-date information in public registers, be able to declare its ownership structure, and conduct an activity permitted by the bank's policy.

For Romanian companies, the applicant enters the unique identification code and checks the retrieved name, registered office, status, and representatives. If the register shows suspension, dissolution, insolvency, or inconsistent administrator information, the online flow stops and the application can continue only after clarification.

The representative submitting the application must be an adult, hold a valid identity document, and have sufficient authority to open the account. A simple power of attorney uploaded to the application does not automatically replace verification of representation rights. Companies with joint representation, ownership chains across several jurisdictions, or beneficial owners who cannot be identified from documents enter manual review.

Romanian limited liability companies, liberal professions, and entities for which the application provides a dedicated flow are normally eligible online. Associations, foundations, trusts, entities with bearer shares, and structures without legal personality may require processing through a branch.

Technical eligibility does not mean acceptance of the relationship. The bank verifies the activity, purpose of the account, source of initial funds, countries involved, beneficial owners, and authorised persons. The applicable business fee schedule must be consulted separately to determine the cost of an eligible application.
