This synthetic document explains what happens when contact or identity verification fails.

An email-verification code expires after ten minutes. The applicant may request a new code. Only the newest code remains valid.

Five incorrect email codes lock email verification for 60 minutes. Creating another application does not bypass the lock.

A video-identification attempt fails when the document is unreadable, the document is unsupported, the liveness task is incomplete, the face comparison is inconclusive, or application data materially conflicts with the document.

The applicant may make three video-identification attempts. After the third unsuccessful attempt, the online video step is locked for exactly 24 hours.

During the lock period, the applicant may wait, choose branch-assisted onboarding, or contact support to understand which evidence is missing. Support cannot manually mark an applicant as verified.

After two failed liveness checks, the applicant may request the optional same-day liveness review described in the current fee schedule. The service does not guarantee approval.

A low-confidence facial comparison should normally create manual review rather than immediate decline. A confirmed false or altered document creates a declined status.

Changing the mobile number after identity verification invalidates the completed verification and requires the new number to be verified. Correcting a minor spelling error does not necessarily require a new video session.

The assistant must distinguish between a temporary technical lock, action required, manual review, and final decline.
