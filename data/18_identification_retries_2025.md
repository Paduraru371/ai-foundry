This policy applies to applications started up to and including 14 January 2026.

An applicant could complete no more than **5 video-identification attempts within a 24-hour period**. An attempt was recorded after the live-presence sequence started, even if the user closed the application before completing it. Repeated photographs of the document before the video sequence started were not counted separately.

After five unsuccessful results, identification was blocked for 24 hours from the last attempt. Support could confirm that the block existed but could not manually reset the counter. When a general technical outage was confirmed, affected attempts could be removed automatically.

The application remained available for **30 calendar days** from the start date. Valid activity, such as confirming a code or uploading a requested document, updated the last-activity date but did not extend the total period beyond 30 days. After expiry, unsigned data could no longer be used for activation and the applicant had to start a new application.

If the identity document expired during the 30-day period, a new document was required before signing. An expired application could not retain the earlier fees and terms if it was restarted after a new version came into force.

This policy is historical. The 2026 policy must be consulted for applications started on or after 15 January 2026.
