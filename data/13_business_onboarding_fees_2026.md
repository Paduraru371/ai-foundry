The fees below apply to services associated with starting a business banking relationship. Operational fees after activation are described in the fee schedule for the selected package.

| Applicant type | Standard opening | Additional representative | Foreign document reviewed | Card delivery |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| SRL Start | 0 lei | 20 lei/person | 75 lei/document | 25 lei |
| SRL Standard | 0 lei | 15 lei/person | 75 lei/document | 0 lei |
| Liberal profession | 0 lei | 20 lei/person | 75 lei/document | 0 lei |
| Company with an extended structure | 150 lei | 25 lei/person | 90 lei/document | 25 lei |
| Online Account 2 in 1 | 0 lei | according to the business component | according to the company type | 0 lei for the first delivery |

A structure is considered extended when it includes at least three ownership levels, an entity registered outside the European Economic Area, or more than five people who must be verified. The classification is communicated before the documents are signed.

The additional-representative fee covers initial identification and permission setup. It does not apply to the legal representative who starts the application. Replacing or adding people after activation is a separate operation.

Review of a foreign document does not include an authorised translation, apostille, or legalisation. If the document cannot be accepted without a translation, the customer must provide it at their own expense.

To answer fully whether a company can open an account and how much it costs, both the business eligibility document and this fee schedule must be consulted. Payment of a review fee does not guarantee approval and does not remove beneficial-owner or source-of-funds checks.
