This synthetic schedule contains invented charges for business-onboarding retrieval tests.

| Service | Start package | Growth package |
|---|---:|---:|
| Account-opening review | 30 RON | 0 RON |
| Verification of each representative after the second | 20 RON | 10 RON |
| Foreign corporate shareholder review | 150 RON per entity | 100 RON per entity |
| Complex ownership review | 250 RON | 175 RON |
| Priority review target | 300 RON | 200 RON |

The first two authorised representatives are included in the standard review. The additional-person charge begins with the third representative.

A foreign corporate shareholder review applies when an entity outside Romania appears in the ownership chain. It is charged per foreign entity, not per document.

A complex ownership review applies where the ownership chain contains more than two corporate layers, voting rights differ from economic ownership, or control is exercised through contractual arrangements. Paying the review fee does not make an otherwise unsupported entity eligible.

The priority-review charge changes the target processing time to five business days after all required documents are received. It does not guarantee approval.

The account-opening review is charged only after the preliminary eligibility assessment passes. It is refunded when the application is declined because of a processing error. It is not refunded when the applicant withdraws after document review begins.

Initial funding amounts do not appear in this file because they are eligibility conditions. A correct total must combine this schedule with the business eligibility document.
