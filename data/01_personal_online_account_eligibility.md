title: Eligibility for a personal account opened online

The online account-opening process is available to individuals who are at least 18 years old, hold a valid identity document, and can complete remote identification using a phone with a functioning camera. The applicant must provide a personal mobile phone number and an email address that they can access directly. Both contact channels are validated with one-time codes before the application can continue.

The applicant must declare all tax residencies, the main purpose of the account, occupation, employer or primary source of income, and the expected range of monthly transactions. A person who already has an active personal current account cannot open a second one through the standard new-customer flow; additional products must be requested through the mobile banking application.

Romanian citizens with an identity document issued in Romania are eligible, as are applicants who present an accepted national identity document together with proof of address when the address is not shown on the document. Applications involving multiple tax residencies, US tax status, politically exposed person status, or an applicant over 70 years old may continue, but they are always subject to additional review.

Applications for minors, people acting through a power of attorney, people under guardianship, or applicants who cannot complete the video verification independently are not processed fully online. In these situations, the application displays instructions for continuing through a bank branch.

Eligibility allows an applicant to start the process but does not guarantee approval. The account is opened only after identity verification, customer due diligence, and signing of the contractual documents.
